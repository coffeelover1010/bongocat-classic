# Credits and attribution

**Bongo Cat character/art:** [@StrayRogue](https://x.com/StrayRogue)

**Original Bongo Cat video concept:** @DitzyFlama

The shipped sprite atlas is rendered from an SVG conversion of the Bongo Cat icon glyphs from [kitgore/BongoCat](https://github.com/kitgore/BongoCat). This free add-on follows the published Bongo Cat FAQ guidance to credit and link the original artist.

## Optional Kitgore icon-font conversion

`BongoCatClassic/ArtSource/KitgoreBongoCatGlyphs/` contains the SVG conversion used to render the shipped texture atlas. The upstream MIT licence is included verbatim in that folder.
