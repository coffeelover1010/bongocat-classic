The shipped sprite atlas is an original, independently drawn outline design. It does not include artwork from the Bongo Cat Buddy VS Code extension or from bongo.cat. This free add-on follows the published Bongo Cat FAQ guidance to credit and link the original artist.

## Optional Kitgore icon-font conversion

`BongoCatClassic/ArtSource/KitgoreBongoCatGlyphs/` contains an unshipped SVG conversion of the icon glyphs from [kitgore/BongoCat](https://github.com/kitgore/BongoCat). They are retained as a reference and are not used by the installed texture atlas. The upstream MIT licence is included verbatim in that folder.
